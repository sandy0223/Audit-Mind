# Spice Route Kitchens — Food Safety House Standards

These are Spice Route's internal standards, based on FSSAI Schedule 4 guidance for food businesses.
Where FSSAI or a client contract is stricter, the stricter rule applies.

## Temperature limits

- Chilled storage (walk-in, reach-in, cold display): at or below 5°C.
- Frozen storage: at or below -18°C. A reading up to -15°C during a defrost cycle must be rechecked within one hour.
- Hot holding (bain-marie, holding cabinet): at or above 63°C. Food below 63°C for more than 2 hours is discarded.
- Cooking: core temperature at least 75°C for poultry and minced meat.
- Walk-in fridges are checked three times a day: opening (06:30), mid-day (14:00) and closing (21:30).

## Receiving deliveries

- Chilled deliveries (poultry, meat, dairy, seafood) must arrive at or below 5°C. Above 5°C → reject and log the rejection.
- The receiver probes the product, records the temperature, their name and the shift on the receiving log.
- No delivery is accepted "in a hurry": the driver waits until the check is done.

## Pest control

- Daily in-house pest check, recorded every day, by the opening shift. A blank day is treated as a missed check.
- Licensed contractor visit (PestGuard) every two weeks, with the service report filed.

## Corrective actions

- Every breach needs a corrective action recorded the same day, with an owner.
- Every corrective action must be followed up: did the fix work? Record the outcome with evidence (e.g. readings for 7 days).

## Audits

- FSSAI food safety officers inspect without fixed schedules.
- Corporate catering clients (e.g. Northwind Corporate Catering) audit outlets that cook for them, and re-check previous findings.
- Auditors usually ask for: temperature logs, receiving logs, pest control logs, cleaning schedules, corrective-action records and staff hygiene training records.
